## Fogueos

Every **Saturday**, Team Delta met to conduct **“fogueos”**, internal simulation sessions designed to replicate  
the **official competition environment** as closely as possible.  

During these sessions, the team performed full test runs following WRO challenge rules and timing.  
This practice allowed us to identify weaknesses in the robot’s design, control algorithms, and team coordination.  

Through each fogueo, we refined our **vehicle’s mechanical setup**, optimized **sensor calibration**,  
and improved **decision-making algorithms**, ensuring that by the time of competition,  
the robot performed with both **consistency and precision** under pressure.
